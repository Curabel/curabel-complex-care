This policy explains how we collect and use personal information when you visit this website or contact us through it. It covers the website only. It does not cover personal information we handle when providing care services. That is explained separately to families, referrers and staff.

### 3. Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you use our enquiry form or email us, we collect:

- Your name
- Your organisation (if any)
- Your email address
- Your phone number
- The contents of your message

**Information collected automatically.** When you visit the site, our hosting provider may record basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and the date and time of your visit. We use this to keep the website secure and working properly.

**Please do not send health information.** Please do not put personal health information about a child or any other person in the enquiry form. If we need it, we will ask for it through a secure route once we are in contact.

### 4. How and why we use your information

| What we do | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Reply to your enquiry and keep a record of it | To respond to referral partners, families and others who contact us | Legitimate interests (running our business and answering enquiries) |
| Keep the website secure and working | To protect the site and fix technical problems | Legitimate interests |
| Meet legal obligations | For example, responding to a request from a regulator | Legal obligation |

We do not use your information for marketing emails unless you ask us to. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.
