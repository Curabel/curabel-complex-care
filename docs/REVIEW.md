# Homepage review and launch requirements

## Research informing the design

Reviewed on 12 November 2026. These are research references, not copied designs or assets.

- [First Option Healthcare](https://firstoptionhc.com/): clear separation of paediatric support, clinical oversight and other audience pathways. Curabel uses a focused child-and-family story and an understated list of eight specialisms.
- [Apollo Home Healthcare](https://www.apollohomehealthcare.com/): emphasis on personalised care at home, clinical quality and routes to recruitment/contact. Curabel balances the family narrative with an explicit care delivery model.
- [Thornbury Community Services](https://www.thornburycommunityservices.co.uk/): distinct journeys for nurses/HCAs and care purchasers, with prominent complex care information. Curabel provides separate family, professional referral and careers enquiry states.

Curabel’s original design uses local editorial fonts, a 55/45-style split hero, generous whitespace, two photographs, line-separated service disclosures, a forest-green care journey and a neutral leadership portrait area. No competitor imagery, copy, partnerships, testimonials, regulatory status or operational claims are reused.

## Before further page development

- Review the homepage typography, palette, photography, section order and responsive presentation.
- Provide the official logo and Caroline McKnight’s approved portrait when available.
- Approve the homepage direction before creating About, Services, Approach, Professionals, Careers or Contact pages.

## Before public launch

- Confirm company legal and registered office details, contact channels and geographic coverage.
- Confirm service availability and operational status; current copy states Curabel is being established.
- Approve clinical copy, care delivery wording, safeguarding information and any regulatory statements with the appropriate professionals.
- Finalise privacy and accessibility statements, including lawful bases, retention, processor/recipient information and accessible contact routes.
- Implement and verify a secure enquiry backend and approved privacy consent. The preview sends nothing.
- Complete assistive technology testing and an accessibility audit. Automated checks are useful but do not establish full WCAG conformance.
- Review stock image suitability and replace with approved client photography where appropriate.
- Configure production hosting/domain, canonical URL and final social metadata, then enable indexing only when approved for launch.

No regulatory identifiers, testimonials, awards, client relationships, salaries, benefits, active vacancies or unconfirmed contact details have been invented.
